Intelligent ticket routing

I built a classifier to assign Freshdesk support tickets and hand uncertain cases back to the support team.

My role

As a machine learning engineer, I engineered over 20 features, compared classification models and helped deploy the inference service.

Approach

I compared logistic regression, random forest and XGBoost, then used SMOTE and tuning to improve macro-F1. The service used FastAPI and Docker on AWS with a 75% confidence threshold for automatic routing.

Evaluation

Macro-F1 summarizes performance across ticket categories. Tuning improved the score from 0.71 to 0.80. The deployed workflow reduced assignment time by a reported 66%.

Baseline macro-F1
0.71
Tuned macro-F1
0.80
Reduction in assignment time
66%

Limitations

Tickets below the confidence threshold went to manual review. Changes in ticket categories or language can affect model performance and the share of tickets that require review.
