Sign language recognition

I built a webcam classifier to recognize fingerspelled letters from the shape of a hand.

Go to the interactive demo

My role

I built the CNN classifier, prepared examples for model development and integrated inference with a webcam loop using OpenCV.

Approach

MediaPipe extracts 21 landmarks from the hand. The classifier uses a representation of those landmarks to estimate the letter, and OpenCV displays the result on the webcam feed.
