Mapping forest canopy height

Satellite imagery can reveal where forests grow. At ISRO, I used it to estimate how tall those forests are.

Go to the interactive demo

My role

As a machine learning engineering intern, I prepared satellite imagery for training and inference, generated canopy-height estimates, and compared predictions against airborne LiDAR.

Approach

I used Rasterio and GDAL to prepare 2,048 × 2,048 image patches, then used DINOv2, DPT and CNN models to generate dense height estimates.

Evaluation

I evaluated the model against LiDAR reference data. The figures below summarize the original project at 25 m scale.

Limitations

The reported accuracy describes the original validation. Differences in forest type, imagery and ground coverage can affect transfer. The interactive map uses public Sentinel-2 imagery and ETH’s canopy-height and uncertainty layers. The LiDAR tool computes validation metrics from the CSV you supply.
